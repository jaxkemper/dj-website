About

Built for the dance floor.

BAUM is an emerging DJ and producer with a sound built around energy, movement, and connection. Blending tech house grooves, bouncy UKG, and elements of bass music, his sets move between familiar records, unexpected selections, and high energy moments designed for the dance floor.

Based between the Bay Area and Tucson, BAUM has built a growing presence across two distinct music communities. What began with a renegade event at the Golden Gate Bridge became the start of a fast growing artist project, leading to sets at some of the Bay Area’s most recognized venues alongside an increasingly notable list of artists.

He approaches every performance with the room in mind. The goal isn’t just to play songs. It’s to read the crowd and leave them remembering how it made them feel.
